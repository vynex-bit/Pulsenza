About Us

INNOVATION

What We Do

Pulsenza focuses on converting everyday human movement into clean, usable energy using piezoelectric technology.

How It Works

When pressure is applied to piezoelectric materials, they generate a small electrical charge that can be harvested and stored.

Why It Matters

By placing energy-harvesting tiles in walkways and public spaces, we can create smarter and more sustainable infrastructure.

Our Goal

To turn wasted motion into useful energy and support the development of practical energy-harvesting solutions.

Power in Motion

Every step you take carries energy that is normally lost. Pulsenza captures that kinetic force through advanced piezoelectric materials and converts it into clean, usable electricity. By embedding these systems into walkways, floors, and public spaces, we turn everyday human movement into a continuous source of renewable power — making infrastructure smarter, more efficient, and more sustainable.
